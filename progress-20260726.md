**Key decisions.**
- Property source: typed-in standard steam-table points (Moran/Cengel) rather than
  CoolProp-at-generation — no extra tooling; provenance visible in the generator.
  Rejected: installing CoolProp ad hoc (adds an unpinned generation-time dependency for
  no accuracy benefit at our 0.5% gate).
- u = h − p·v derived in the generator instead of transcribed (fewer typo surfaces);
  consistency test asserts the independently-fit u/h/v agree < 5e-4.
- Added low-range fits beyond the plan's two families: hf_t(T), rho_f_t(T), tsat_lowp(p)
  — needed for condensate/DA enthalpy, pump density, NPSH; recorded as HANDOFF D2.
- Kelvin throughout the API (HANDOFF D3); analytic d/dp emitted for the drum Newton
  (HANDOFF D4), FD-verified.
- Fit forms: degree-4 polys in z = ln(p/8 MPa) for the drum family, v_g as exp(poly)
  for uniform relative error; ln(psat) poly in T[K]; degree-3 for hf(T), vf(T).
