**Key decisions** (full rationale in HANDOFF.md D5–D10):
- D5 pegging steam closes DA energy balance — firing at rated 199.59 MW vs plan table's
  ≈180 MW (table formula omits the 23.4 MW DA heating). Rejected: booking it as a
  declared unmodeled audit input (hides a real drum steam draw).
- D6 droop law scaled by x_rated=0.85 (plan's raw law conflicts with k_t calibration);
  k_t = P_rated/(x_rated·w_spec·p_drum) = 1.131222e-5 kg/(s·Pa) exactly.
- D7 root formulation: actuator rows degenerate + hotwell/DA mass rows linearly
  dependent → replaced with loop-equilibrium + hotwell-level-pinned rows (found
  empirically: first solve converged to hotwell +833 mm on the free family).
- D9 chest holdup k_t·τ_ch·p_chest counted in mass audit (exact, ≈31 kg at rated).
- D10 turbine trip opens generator breaker → bus dead → pumps trip (M2 wiring).
- FP NPSH_req flat (a=9.8, b=59.6) so DA drain-down crosses the 0.5 m margin ≈95 s
  after condensate loss, inside the 45–120 s first-trip window, before tank-dry.
